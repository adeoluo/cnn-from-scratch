# Data Card - CNN From Scratch

## Source
- **Fully synthetic.** Images are generated in-process by a seeded random generator. There is no
  external dataset, download, or network access.

## Composition
- Each image is a single-channel `size x size` grid (default 8x8) with low-amplitude uniform
  noise and one bright oriented stroke of length three placed at a random position.
- Three balanced classes: horizontal, vertical, and diagonal strokes.
- Default splits: 120 training and 90 held-out images, generated from separate seed streams.

## Labels and supervision
- The label is the stroke orientation. Supervision is standard cross-entropy; there are no human
  labels.

## Provenance and licensing
- Generated by this repository's code under the MIT license. No third-party data is included.

## Filtering and contamination
- Train and test images are drawn from different seed streams, so the held-out set is independent.

## Known limitations
- The images are small and synthetic by design so convolution's advantage is measurable and
  training is fast. The distribution is not meant to resemble natural images.
